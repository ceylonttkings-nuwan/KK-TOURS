Iconic Sri Lanka Destinations

We cover all major highlight spots across the island during your 6, 9, 12, or 14 days private tour with seamless door-to-door transportation.

Cultural Triangle

Sigiriya Lion Rock

5th Century ancient sky fortress with royal gardens and historic frescoes.

Central Highlands

Ella & Nine Arch Bridge

World famous scenic blue train trip through misty tea plantations and waterfalls.

Wildlife Safari

Yala National Park

Home to the highest density of wild leopards, elephants, sloth bears & exotic birds.

Southern Coast

Mirissa & Galle Fort

Golden tropical sandy beaches, Blue Whale boat expeditions, and UNESCO Dutch Fort.

Sacred Heritage

Kandy Sacred City

The Temple of the Sacred Tooth Relic, Botanical Gardens & traditional cultural shows.

Little England

Nuwara Eliya

Colonial style tea estates, Gregory Lake boat rides & authentic Ceylon tea tasting.
